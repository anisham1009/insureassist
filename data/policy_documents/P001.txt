Policy Name: HealthSecure Basic
Policy ID: P001
Policy Type: Health Insurance

Coverage:
This policy provides health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh.

Eligibility:
Customers between 18 and 60 years of age are eligible subject to policy terms.

Annual Premium:
₹8,500.

Deductible:
₹10,000.

Waiting Period:
The initial waiting period is 30 days.

Pre-existing Diseases:
Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 4 years.

Maternity:
Maternity coverage is not included.

Hospitalization:
Hospitalization expenses are covered according to policy terms.

Room Rent:
Single AC room is covered subject to applicable limits.

Claim Process:
Customers can submit claims through the online claim process.

Exclusions:
Non-medical expenses are excluded.
