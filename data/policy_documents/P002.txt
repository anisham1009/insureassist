Policy Name: HealthSecure Plus
Policy ID: P002
Policy Type: Health Insurance

Coverage:
This policy provides health insurance coverage up to ₹10 lakh.

Eligibility:
Customers between 18 and 65 years of age are eligible subject to policy terms.

Annual Premium:
₹12,500.

Deductible:
₹5,000.

Waiting Period:
The initial waiting period is 30 days.

Pre-existing Diseases:
Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 3 years.

Maternity:
Maternity coverage is available after the applicable waiting period.

Hospitalization:
Hospitalization expenses are covered according to policy terms.

Room Rent:
There is no room-rent limit subject to policy conditions.

Claim Process:
Customers can submit claims through the online claim process.

Exclusions:
Cosmetic procedures are excluded unless covered under applicable policy conditions.
